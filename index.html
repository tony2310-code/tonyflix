CONTENT REQUESTS

Can’t Find It? Just Ask!

Each active TonyFlix subscription can request up to 5 movies, or up to 5 individual TV episodes, or 1 complete TV season at a time.

These limits apply per subscription, not per device. All devices on the same subscription share one request allowance.

Movies and individual episodes: Once your requested movies or episodes have been added and you have finished watching them, you can request up to 5 more movies or 5 more individual episodes.

Complete TV seasons: Once you have finished watching your requested season, let TonyFlix know before requesting the next season.

There is no monthly request quota. Requests depend on content availability and available storage.
